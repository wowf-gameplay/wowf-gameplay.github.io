WoW Forever Gameplay Sims

Practise a level 60 DPS rotation in your browser. You press the abilities; the sim swings at a raid boss dummy, tracks your resources and cooldowns, and shows a Warcraft Logs-style breakdown when the dummy dies.

About the sims

  • Every sim has toggles for raid buffs, consumables and boss debuffs, a Gear tab, a Talents tab with hover tooltips, and a Stats tab for entering your own character stats.
  • Keys are rebindable (with Shift and Ctrl), dummy health is adjustable, and the end-of-fight breakdown mirrors a Warcraft Logs damage table.
  • Default gear, enchants and talent builds follow the MythicSim tier-list presets. Mechanics follow the open-source engine MythicSim is built on and Forever beta tooltips, and may change as the beta does.
  • Source code: github.com/wowf-gameplay. Each sim is a single HTML file with no build step.
